Keep or Delete includes a vendored browser build of @vladmandic/face-api and its
face detection, landmark, and descriptor model files for offline use.

Project: @vladmandic/face-api
License: MIT
License text: face-api-LICENSE.txt

No network service is required by the face grouping feature. The model files are
loaded from the installed application package.
